Dialogue inside quotation marks is read by the character who says it. The reader works out speakers from tags like “said Mara” or “Tobias asked,” and from who acts in the same paragraph. Words wrapped in *asterisks* or _underscores_ count as italics, and whole italic sentences are read as speech or thoughts. You can also open a PDF, an EPUB eBook, a Word (.docx) file, a web page or a text file, or drag one onto this page.

Get clearer, more natural voices

Your device only has basic voices installed. They’re free to upgrade:

iPhone or iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (or Spoken Content) → Voices → English. Download a few marked Premium or Enhanced, like Ava, Zoe, Evan and Nathan. Then reopen Hearthtale.

Computer: use the downloaded Hearthtale file with Natural voices, or open it in Microsoft Edge.

Pick a voice for each role. If a line has the wrong speaker, change the name beside it, or choose “+ New character”. If two entries are one person, use “Same person as”.
